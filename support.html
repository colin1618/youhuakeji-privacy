常见问题

日记会保存在哪里？

默认保存在当前设备。开启 iCloud 同步后，会保存到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库。更改同步开关后需重启应用；同时确认设备已登录 iCloud、网络可用，并且两端使用同一 Apple 账户。

怎样添加城市与天气？

编辑今天的日记，点「地点与天气」，再选择获取当前位置天气。定位权限只在这项操作时请求。你也可以拒绝定位，并手动填写城市和天气。

Pro 购买没有生效怎么办？

先确认设备登录了用于购买的 Apple 账户，并检查网络连接；然后在 Pro 页面使用「恢复购买」。订阅管理、取消和退款由 Apple 账户订阅页面处理。

怎样保护或恢复日记？

可在「设置」>「安全」中开启应用锁，也可在单篇日记的更多操作里锁定日记。误删的记录可在「回收站」恢复；永久删除后不能在应用内恢复。

Frequently asked questions

Where are my entries stored?

Entries are stored on your device by default. If you enable iCloud sync, they are stored in your private iCloud database. Restart the app after changing the sync setting. Make sure iCloud is signed in, the network is available, and both devices use the same Apple Account.

How do I add a city and weather?

Edit today's entry, tap “Location and weather,” then choose the option to fetch current weather. Location permission is requested only for this action. You can deny access and enter the city and weather manually.

What if my Pro purchase is missing?

Check that the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase and that you have a network connection. Then use “Restore Purchases” on the Pro page. Apple handles subscription management, cancellation, and refunds.

How do I protect or restore an entry?

Turn on the app lock in Settings > Security, or lock one entry from its More Actions menu. Recover deleted entries from Recently Deleted. Entries permanently deleted there cannot be restored in the app.

When contacting us

Please include your device model, operating-system version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message. Do not email your Apple Account password or unredacted private journal content.
